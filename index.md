# RyeFlix Video — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

RyeFlix Video is a TV app that shows the films of a Plex server as a video store, and its shows as TV channels (TV Rye). This policy tells you what data the app uses, and where that data goes.

## The short version

- The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- The app does not sell or share your data.
- The developer does not get or keep any of your data.

## Data that stays on your TV

- **Your Plex sign-in.** When you sign in with plex.tv/link, the app keeps a Plex access token on the TV. The app uses it only to talk to Plex.
- **Your settings.** Saved shelves, shelf order, the channels you skip, and your list of ordered films stay on the TV.
- **Film covers.** The app keeps copies of film covers on the TV, so that the store opens fast.

To remove all of this data, uninstall the app, or clear its storage in the TV settings.

## Data that goes to other services

| Service | What the app sends | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Plex (plex.tv and your Plex server) | Your Plex token and the requests for your library | To show your films and play them |
| Anthropic (the Claude API) | Only the words that you type or say to Pop, the store clerk | To get film suggestions. Anthropic does not get your name, your Plex account or your library. |
| The film request server of the library owner (Seerr), if set up | Your Plex token and the film you ask for | To ask the library owner to add a film |
| Wikipedia and Wikimedia | Requests for public pages and posters | For the "In Memoriam" and "Trending Now" shelves |
| ipwho.is or ipapi.co | Your internet address (all websites see it) | To find your rough town for the weather on the storefront |
| Open-Meteo | The rough location of your town | To get the weather |

Each service has its own privacy policy.

## The microphone

The app asks for the microphone permission, so that you can tell Pop what you want to see. The app listens only when you start it. The app sends the words, not the recording, to Anthropic. The app does not keep recordings.

## Children

RyeFlix Video is not made for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect data from children.
